## SECTION 2 · WHAT WE LEARNED

**Headline:** What we learned.
**Subhead:** Three findings make the rest of this plan inevitable.

**Section intro (right column):**
We reviewed five competitors, two paid social audits, and a full local SEO read of Bunkr's footprint inside the Perimeter. The research kept circling back to the same three things.

**Finding 01: The category is split into two lanes.**
Five Iron, Fair Way Social, and Atlanta Golf & Social sell golf as a social outing. Back Nine and Golf Envy sell tech and performance credentials. Bunkr's brand sits in Lane A. The current paid social spend is built for Lane B. We are advertising to a different audience than the one the brand is for.

**Finding 02: The Perimeter paid social space is empty.**
Atlanta Golf & Social has 213 Facebook page likes and almost no paid activity. Back Nine is just landing. Fair Way Social is in Alpharetta with a multi-sport story that dilutes its golf identity. Inside the Perimeter, nobody owns paid social. Whoever moves first claims it.

**Finding 03: Nobody is doing retargeting.**
Every cold-ad visitor who does not convert on the first click is being abandoned by every local competitor. Retargeting is a same-week unlock and nobody in our market is doing it.

**Pull quote (closes the section):**
> We win by owning the local funnel and the local search anchor. Then Bunkr's team builds on top.
> — Marketwake POV

## SECTION 3 · FIVE COMPETITORS, AT A GLANCE

**Headline:** Five competitors. *At a glance.*

**Section intro (right column):**
Two real threats. Two barely a threat. One worth studying as a model. The marketing implication for Bunkr sits next to each.

### Card 1 · Five Iron Golf
**Threat label:** Highest National Threat
**Positioning:** "It's All Good Form." Agency-produced 15-second Meta spots. Best-in-class paid creative in the category. Downtown Atlanta location.
**For Bunkr:** We don't compete on production value. We compete on proximity and a tighter funnel. They're 25 minutes south of the Perimeter.

### Card 2 · Fair Way Social
**Threat label:** Highest Local Threat
**Positioning:** Alpharetta, 8 miles north. Multi-sport entertainment venue. Zombie dodgeball plus golf plus restaurant. Strong group event focus.
**For Bunkr:** We are pure golf. They split attention across sports. Crew planners who specifically want golf come to us. Inside the Perimeter we're 15 minutes closer than they are.

### Card 3 · Back Nine Golf
**Threat label:** Franchise, Expanding
**Positioning:** "Where Golf Never Sleeps." Functional positioning, not aspirational. Just landing in Atlanta.
**For Bunkr:** They lead with tech. We lead with experience. Our paid funnel and local search anchor beat their utility positioning.

### Card 4 · Atlanta Golf & Social
**Threat label:** Local Benchmark
**Positioning:** 3 bays in Chamblee. Closed-membership waitlist signals real demand. 213 Facebook page likes. Earned local press coverage (Explore Georgia, atlgolfs.com, Adventure Collective) without paid spend.
**For Bunkr:** The model worth studying. We run the paid and search game they aren't running.

### Card 5 · Golf Envy
**Threat label:** Future Threat
**Positioning:** California-based, GOLFZON Tour champions. Not in Atlanta yet. Active in local Atlanta golf community.
**For Bunkr:** They will arrive. The Perimeter share of voice we build before they get here is the moat.

### Blue summary block at bottom: "What this means for Bunkr's marketing."

- **We win on local + paid.** Five Iron is downtown, Fair Way is north, Back Nine is utility, AGS has no paid spend. Inside the Perimeter the paid space is empty.
- **We win on retargeting.** None of the local competitors are running it. Same-week unlock the day we ship the funnel.
- **We win on local search.** AGS is the only competitor with a serious local citation footprint, and they still leave the local pack open. GBP plus 70+ directory sync wins it.
- **We win on the independent identity.** Two real, named owners is a moat against the franchises. How Bunkr's team builds on it lives in the bonus playbook below.

## SECTION 5 · CHANNEL ONE · META PAID SOCIAL

**Headline:** Channel one. *Meta paid social.*
**Subhead:** A real funnel. Three layers. Three ICPs.

**Section intro:**
The account today is 100% conversion targeting 2.6M to 3M cold people inside a 15-mile radius. We are flipping it. Three layers, three audiences, three creative tracks. Each ICP gets its own ad set. The geography tightens. The retargeting runs.

### Funnel card 01 · Awareness
**Stage label:** Layer 01 · Awareness
**Percentage:** 25-30% of spend
**Title:** Fill the retargeting pool.
**Description:** Video-first, static and design in support. Facility tours, Trackman moments, group fun, lifestyle stills. 15-mile geo around Dunwoody to cast a wider net at the top of the funnel. Advantage+ audience expansion off. The goal here is not bookings. It is reach and ThruPlay.
**KPI:** Cost per ThruPlay, video view rate

### Funnel card 02 · Consideration (dark card)
**Stage label:** Layer 02 · Consideration
**Percentage:** 20-25% of spend
**Title:** Move warm audiences down the path.
**Description:** Retargets video viewers and page engagers. Membership explainers, "first time at a simulator" content, event recaps. The goal here is landing page views and warm intent.
**KPI:** Cost per landing page view

### Funnel card 03 · Conversion (blue card)
**Stage label:** Layer 03 · Conversion
**Percentage:** 50% of spend
**Title:** Convert hot, not cold.
**Description:** Bay bookings and membership signups. Hot retargeting only. 14 to 30-day website visitors, booking-page abandoners. Hard CTAs, urgency, first-session offers. Not broad cold targeting.
**KPI:** Cost per booking

### Pixel callout (pink box)

**Tag:** Fix First
**Headline:** The pixel problem we have to solve before we scale.
**Body:** The Meta pixel is on Bunkr's main domain. The booking flow lives on yourgolfbooking.com. Today, we cannot track post-click conversions. Every dollar we spend on paid social is partially blind. Either we move the pixel onto the booking domain or we move the booking flow onto Bunkr's domain. The whole paid plan rests on this.

### Black summary block: "What changes from day one."

- **Geography tightens as we move down the funnel.** 15-mile radius for awareness, tightens to 10 for consideration and conversion. Advantage+ audience expansion turns off.
- **Audiences split.** Three ad sets (Native, Commuter, Crew Planner), not one broad campaign.
- **Retargeting goes live.** Website visitors (14 and 30 day windows), video viewers, page engagers.
- **Video-first, static in support.** 9:16 video is the primary format. Static and design ads run alongside to reinforce the message and hold the feed when video isn't the right play.

## SECTION 6 · CREATIVE DIRECTION · DESIGN

**Headline:** Creative direction. *The look.*
**Subhead:** Moodboard first. Mockups second.
**Section intro:** Every paid ad has to live on a feed next to organic posts. The creative direction is built to feel native to Instagram and Meta first, polished second. Lifestyle-forward, golf-coded, anti-country-club. Built to resonate, not announce. Bunkr's voice, not an agency's.

### Three creative pillars

**Pillar 01 · Lifestyle, not sport.**
Golf is the backdrop. The story is the people, the night, the crew, the bar, the bay. A community brand, not a sport brand. The swing comes second.
*Keyword chip:* Community

**Pillar 02 · Local, not corporate.**
Dunwoody specific. Perimeter specific. Independent and named, not a franchise. Built to resonate with neighbors, not impress tourists. The brand feels like someone you already know.
*Keyword chip:* Resonate

**Pillar 03 · Nostalgic, not vintage.**
There's a difference between vintage and nostalgic. Vintage describes what is old. Nostalgia describes how it makes us feel. One is about age, the other about memory and meaning. Bunkr lives in the second. Tactile, authentic, lived-in. Every static and video should feel like a memory in the making, not a stock photo.
*Keyword chip:* Nostalgic · Tactile · Authentic

### The moodboard

**Subhead:** The moodboard.
**Vibe target:** Nostalgic, tactile, lived-in. Country-club energy crossed with neighborhood bar warmth. Trackman, leather, brass, denim, dim light. The kind of place that feels authentic, not staged. Where the brand lives.
**Caption tag:** Moodboard · v1
**Caption keywords:** Nostalgic · Tactile · Community · Authentic

## SECTION 7 · CHANNEL TWO · GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE

**Headline:** Channel two. *Google Business Profile.*
**Subhead:** The local search anchor. We own this.

**Section intro:**
GBP is the most underused asset Bunkr has. It is claimed, but underbuilt. No primary category locked, few photos, no Q&A seeded, no services listed, no posts. Underneath it, the site has broken footer NAP and no schema. All of that is fixable, and the local pack is winnable.

### The Goal (lime box, big "Top 3" number)

**Tag:** The Goal
**Big number:** Top 3 (in the local pack)
**Body:** "Indoor golf Dunwoody." "Golf simulator Atlanta Perimeter." "Golf simulator near me" inside the Perimeter. Owning the local pack for these three searches is the GBP outcome we're optimizing for.

### Seven levers list (right side of GBP section)

**01 · Categories locked.**
Primary: Indoor golf course. Secondaries: Sports bar, Event venue, Recreation center. Each secondary is a separate ranking signal.

**02 · 20+ photos to start.**
Bay close-ups, food and drink, group shots, exterior signage, a short walk-through video. Every new addition to the space gets photographed and added that week.

**03 · Q&A seeded in the founders' voice.**
BYO clubs? Food? Parking? Memberships? Lessons? Mirror the actual searches people run. Owner-tagged answers carry weight.

**04 · Services listed.**
Bay rental, memberships, private events, corporate, birthdays, lessons. Each one is a ranking signal.

**05 · Weekly posts.**
Events, leagues, watch parties, member spotlights, seasonal packages. Freshness is its own ranking signal.

**06 · Attributes set.**
BYO clubs, group events, wheelchair accessible, free parking, accepts reservations. These show up in filtered searches.

**07 · Listing Management underneath.**
Pushes Bunkr's NAP to 70+ directories from one dashboard, then keeps it consistent. Twenty hours of manual work done in one move.

## SECTION 9 · BONUS 01 · THE FOUNDER PLAYBOOK

**Bonus banner:** Bonus 01 · For Bunkr's team

**Headline:** The Founder Playbook.
**Subhead:** What Bunkr's team owns. The behaviors that compound on top.

**Section intro:**
Marketwake delivers the paid funnel and the local search anchor above. This section is the work that sits outside our scope but determines whether the rest compounds or evaporates. Three behaviors, one review goal, and one rule. None of it is glamorous. All of it is owner-led.

### Play card 01 · Film constantly.
**Tag:** Six days a week
**Body:** Five Iron pays M&C Saatchi agency money to look authentic. The local counter to that is being authentic. Turn the camera around. Walk the space. Talk about Tuesday's league, the new menu item, what just happened in bay 3.
**Bullet list:**
- Walkthrough Reels, 60 seconds, no script.
- Member spotlights with real names.
- Event hype: Masters, Ryder Cup, watch parties.
- Trackman moments. Member crushes one. Post it.
- Before/after of the space. New bay. New decor. New menu.

### Play card 02 · Push reviews. Owner-led.
**Tag:** The local search lever
**Body:** Reviews are not a marketing task. They are a hospitality task. At the end of every session, the staff or the founders ask. QR code at every bay landing on the GBP review link. One-sentence script.
**Bullet list:**
- "If you had a good time, the biggest thing you can do for us is leave a Google review. Takes 30 seconds."
- 25 reviews in the first 30 days.
- 8 to 10 per month after.
- QR cards printed, mounted, staff trained on the ask.

### Play card 03 · Be the face. People buy from people.
**Tag:** The local moat
**Body:** AI search and local search both reward named, real, quotable owners. Ashton and Jonl are real. Use that. The franchises cannot say "the owner bartends here." Bunkr can.
**Bullet list:**
- Founder bios on the site.
- Founder voice in the GBP Q&A.
- Founder face in two-thirds of the Reels.
- Founder quotes anywhere the brand shows up in press.

### Creative brief banner (black box, sits above the six shot cards)

**Eyebrow:** The Creative Brief

**Body:** Six core shots to keep on rotation. Founder-led where possible. UGC vibe over polish. If it looks like an ad, it's wrong.
